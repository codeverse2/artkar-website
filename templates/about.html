About Me

Welcome to the creative world of Shruti Raut, the multidisciplinary artist behind Artkar. Driven by a passion for versatility and fine detail, Shruti seamlessly blends the raw precision of pencil graphite realism with the vivid energy of watercolors, intricate mandala art, and striking glow art experiments. Every piece is a testament to patience, technique, and a relentless pursuit of creative boundary-pushing. Whether capturing the human eye or weaving traditional patterns, Artkar turns blank pages into vibrant visual journeys.
